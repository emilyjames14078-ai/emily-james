The Silent Room

A mysterious journey through memory, secrets and the things left unsaid.

Chapter One

The Beginning

The room had been empty for years. Yet every evening, someone left the light on.

Emily stood outside the door, listening to the silence on the other side. She knew she should walk away.

Instead, she reached for the handle.

Chapter Two

The Door

Nothing inside the room looked unusual. There was only an old chair, a dusty table, and a photograph facing the wall.

When she turned the photograph around, she realized she had seen the room before.
